\section*{Supporting documentation and source notes}
\noindent\textbf{Dataset and provenance.} Open Air Chicago Day Aggregations is published by the City of Chicago. The project was developed by the Chicago Department of Public Health (CDPH) and the UIC School of Public Health (SPH), supported by an advisory board, neighborhood/grassroots organizations, and government partners. It provides more localized PM\textsubscript{2.5} and NO\textsubscript{2} information than the federal air-quality-index network alone. The publisher documents 277 non-regulatory network sensors and additional monitors at federal sites. Dr. Serap Erdal at UIC SPH received NIST and RHP Risk Management / ComEd / Exelon funding.\par
\vspace{7pt}
\noindent\textbf{Collection and missing data.} Each row is the mean for one sensor from 00:00 to 23:59:59 UTC. All network sensors monitor PM\textsubscript{2.5} and NO\textsubscript{2}; six also have black-carbon/weather modules, although the black-carbon modules were offline when the data launched. Temperature, humidity and wind are direct sensor values. For PM\textsubscript{2.5} and NO\textsubscript{2}, \texttt{.raw} is uncalibrated and \texttt{.value} is cloud-calibrated. Invalidated measurements are not in the public aggregate; separate high-resolution readings are publicly published, while invalidated data require a request.\par
\vspace{7pt}
\noindent\textbf{Analytical limitations.} The file captures reported, quality-checked sensor-days, not every place or personal exposure in Chicago. The observations cannot establish causation between income, emissions and health. These non-regulatory monitors should not be treated as legal compliance evidence or exact personal-exposure measures. Decisions based on these records could especially affect residents of heavily industrialized or polluted neighborhoods.\par
\vspace{7pt}
\noindent\textbf{Access, terms, and reuse.} CSV and API access are public on the Chicago Data Portal. Our group accessed the portal on October 27, 2026. No dataset-specific license was identified in the team's materials. The City's disclaimer requires derivative applications to display the notice below and honor any additional agency terms; do not assume unconditional rights to relicense processed data. The dataset is refreshed daily.\par
